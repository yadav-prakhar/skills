# Careers sourcing — official domains, what each confirms, and the traps

Every Careers fact printed must be checked on one of these domains **on the day of the run**. A number from a coaching site is a lead, not a fact.

## Domain map

| domain | confirms |
|---|---|
| `csirhrdg.res.in` | Joint CSIR-UGC NET notice: apply window, late-fee window, correction window, exam dates, fee slabs, age limits, subject codes. Public notices sit under `SiteContent/ManagedContent/ATContent/`. |
| `nta.ac.in`, `csirnet.nta.nic.in`, `ugcnet.nta.nic.in` | NTA-run exams: UGC-NET and CSIR-NET applications, extensions, corrigenda, information bulletins, answer keys, results. |
| `ugc.gov.in` | UGC regulations, minimum qualifications for Assistant Professor, recruitment notifications. |
| `ctet.nic.in` | CTET edition number, exam dates, correction window, city slip and admit-card timing, results. |
| `upessc.up.gov.in`, `apply.upessc.org` | UPESSC exams: TGT (Graduate Pay Scale), PRT, PGT, Head Master. The portal's “Important Updates” block carries advertisement number, apply window and exam dates. |
| `uppsc.up.nic.in` | UPPSC advertisements (PCS, RO/ARO), district information slips, admit cards, document-verification notices, corrigenda. |
| `upsssc.gov.in` | UPSSSC PET (advertisement number, exam dates, admit cards) and subordinate-service recruitment. |
| `ssc.gov.in`, `upsc.gov.in` | Central government notifications, calendars, admit cards. |
| `upeducation.gov.in`, `upmsp.edu.in` | UP education department circulars; UP Board (High School/Intermediate) date sheets and practical schedules. |
| `dtu.ac.in`, `svnit.ac.in`, `iith.ac.in/careers` | University Assistant Professor advertisements: advertisement number, department-wise vacancy split, closing date. |

## Traps

- **CSIR-NET and UGC-NET are different exams** with different subjects, fees and dates. Both matter to this reader; never merge them into one item.
- CSIR-NET has a **late-fee window** (₹500) after the main deadline; UGC-NET's extension came **without** a late fee. Read the specific notice.
- CTET's edition number, not the month, is the reliable identifier — the 22nd edition moved from September to December 2026. There is no late entry once both windows close.
- UPESSC TGT reservation is counted **per school**, because aided schools are single units. Do not describe it as a state-wide pool.
- A short notice and the detailed advertisement can disagree (UGC-NET's did in October 2026). Follow the **later** document and say which one you used.
- `upessc.up.nic.in` resolves to a private address and is blocked; the live hosts are `upessc.up.gov.in` and `apply.upessc.org`.

## Worked example — 2026-10-28 (re-verify every issue, never reuse)

- **CSIR-UGC NET Dec 2026**: apply 7 Oct–5 Nov (11.50 p.m.); late fee 6–9 Nov (₹500); correction 10–12 Nov; exam 19/22/27 Dec; ₹1,150 GEN, ₹600 EWS/OBC-NCL, ₹325 SC/ST/PwD/third gender; JRF ≤30 years, no upper age for Assistant Professor; five subjects including Chemical Sciences and Life Sciences & Biotechnology.
- **UGC-NET Dec 2026**: deadline extended to 31 Oct, no late fee; correction 2–4 Nov; exam 14–18 and 21 Dec.
- **UPESSC TGT 2026**, Advt 07/2026: 17,735 posts (16,193 boys, 1,542 girls); apply 24 Oct–23 Nov; correction to 26 Nov; exam 23–24 Dec; Level 7 ₹44,900–₹1,42,400; 120 MCQ / 360 marks / 2 hours, −1 per wrong answer.
- **UPPSC RO/ARO 2026**, Advt A-4/E-1/2026: 501 posts; 26 Oct–26 Nov; correction 3 Dec; graduate + NIELIT 'O' Level; 21–40 years; ₹125/₹65/₹25.
- **DTU**, Advt 04/2026: 219 Assistant Professor posts, including Applied Chemistry 4, Environmental Science & Engineering 6, Biotechnology 6, Applied Physics 15; 23 Oct–7 Dec.
- **CTET**: 22nd edition 12–13 Dec 2026; city slip ~15 days before, admit card 2 days before; 23rd edition not announced.

If a figure above cannot be re-confirmed on the official domain on the day, print `not announced — check <domain>` or mark it `[unconfirmed]`. Do not carry a number forward on trust.
