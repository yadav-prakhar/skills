# Personal life review

- **Period:** [Month / quarter and year]
- **Date completed:** [Date]

This is a reflection, not a report card. Be honest and specific without turning the review into self-criticism.

## 1. Overall sense

- What has felt good or meaningful lately?
- What has felt heavy, neglected, or out of balance?
- What am I grateful for that I have not been giving enough attention?
- What has changed since the last review?

## 2. Important relationships and family

- Which relationships have I cared for well?
- Is there a conversation, repair, boundary, or act of appreciation I should not keep postponing?
- Am I balancing personal commitments and family responsibilities fairly?

## 3. Mental and emotional well-being

- What emotions or stressors have been most present?
- What has helped me feel grounded or supported?
- Is a recurring pattern becoming clearer?
- Do I need support beyond what I can reasonably handle alone?

## 4. Physical health and fitness

- How have sleep, movement, nutrition, energy, and recovery been?
- What is improving?
- Is pain, fatigue, or another concern being ignored?
- What one sustainable adjustment would help?

## 5. Personal finance

- Am I clear on cash flow, obligations, savings, and upcoming expenses?
- Did my spending reflect my priorities?
- Is there a risk, debt, or administrative task I should address?

## 6. Friends, community, and experiences

- Who do I want to make more time for?
- What enjoyable or meaningful experience did I make room for?
- What have I been postponing unnecessarily?

## 7. Identity, values, and purpose

- When did my actions reflect the person I want to be?
- Where did I fall short, and what repair or learning is possible?
- What feels meaningful beyond achievement or external approval?

## 8. The next period

- **Keep:** What is working and deserves to continue?
- **Change:** What should I adjust or stop?
- **Start:** What one small action would make a meaningful difference?
- **Protect:** What relationship, routine, or time needs to be defended?
- **Enjoy:** What am I looking forward to?
