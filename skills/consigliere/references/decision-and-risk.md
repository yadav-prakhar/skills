# Professional decisions, timing, and risk

Use for high-stakes career choices, conflict strategy, negotiations, escalation, role changes, or exits.

## 1. Frame the decision

State the choice, desired outcome, deadline, cost of waiting, and what happens by default. Include the option to do nothing or run a smaller experiment where realistic.

## 2. Evaluate options

Compare a small set of viable options against relevant criteria:
- Strategic fit and long-term career value.
- Relationship and reputation effects.
- Financial and practical consequences.
- Safety, legal, policy, or ethical exposure.
- Reversibility and future optionality.
- Time, energy, and opportunity cost.

Do not use arbitrary numerical scores to create false precision. State where judgment is subjective.

## 3. Pre-mortem

Imagine the chosen plan failed. Identify plausible causes, ignored signals, weak assumptions, and preventive actions. Consider the cost of the alternative too.

## 4. Counter-move analysis

For important actions, consider likely reactions from the decision-maker, affected peers, manager, and relevant functions. Separate plausible reactions from speculative worst cases. Define a response for the one or two most consequential scenarios.

## 5. Timing

Consider decision cycles, budget windows, review periods, project milestones, leadership availability, evidence maturity, emotional temperature, and external deadlines. Timing should improve clarity and options, not become an excuse for indefinite delay.

## 6. Risk controls

Where appropriate:
- Confirm expectations and decisions in writing.
- Keep factual records in approved channels.
- Avoid unnecessary disclosure.
- Seek a second perspective for high-stakes decisions.
- Define a trigger for escalation or exit.
- Preserve professional obligations and handover quality.

## 7. Decide and revisit

State the recommendation, accepted downside, first action, review trigger, and evidence that would change the plan. Do not reopen the decision without a meaningful change in facts or constraints.
