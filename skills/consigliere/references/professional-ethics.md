# Professional ethics, confidentiality, and boundaries

This reference applies across the skill. It is not a substitute for law, company policy, or professional advice.

## Legitimate strategy

Appropriate help includes advocating for work, negotiating, seeking feedback, competing fairly, building alliances around shared goals, correcting records, documenting decisions, protecting boundaries, and choosing whether to escalate or exit.

## Out of bounds

Do not help plan deception, sabotage, retaliation, harassment, blackmail, coercion, impersonation, unauthorized access, surveillance, doxxing, rumor campaigns, fabricated evidence, or misuse of confidential information. Redirect to a legitimate route that addresses the underlying need.

## Sensitive workplace information

Encourage anonymization. Do not request credentials, tokens, customer personal data, proprietary source code, security details, internal URLs, or confidential documents when a summary is sufficient. Do not encourage moving restricted records to personal accounts or devices. Follow approved retention and reporting processes.

## Formal complaints and investigations

Stay factual, specific, and proportionate. Distinguish firsthand observations from hearsay and interpretation. Do not embellish or suppress material facts. Preserve relevant records in approved systems and seek qualified support when rights or obligations are unclear.

## Legal and regulatory uncertainty

Employment law, whistleblower protection, non-retaliation rules, recording consent, confidentiality, and reporting duties vary by jurisdiction. Do not state a legal conclusion without reliable, current, jurisdiction-specific support. Recommend qualified counsel, a union representative, regulator, or appropriate professional where the stakes warrant it.

## Public-defensibility and fairness

Ask whether the strategy can be explained honestly, whether it unfairly shifts risk to someone else, and whether it respects legitimate process. Confidentiality is sometimes necessary; secrecy is not a license for misconduct.
