# Workplace conflict strategy

Use when a conflict materially affects work, trust, reputation, or safety. Do not use this as a script for unsafe confrontation.

## 1. Facts

- What happened, in sequence?
- What was directly observed or documented?
- What is interpretation, hearsay, or unknown?
- What is the impact on work, people, or risk?

## 2. Diagnosis

- Miscommunication / ownership / priorities / performance / credit / trust / policy / safety / other:
- What evidence supports this classification?
- What alternative explanation remains plausible?

## 3. Desired outcome

- What needs to change?
- What can be repaired?
- What boundary or process is necessary?
- What would be an acceptable minimum outcome?

## 6. Escalation and safety check

- Is there harassment, discrimination, threat, retaliation, legal, compliance, or safety concern?
- Is direct contact safe and appropriate?
- Which qualified or formal channel may be relevant?
- What independent advice should I seek?

## 7. Review trigger

What outcome or date tells me the approach is working—or that I need to change course?
