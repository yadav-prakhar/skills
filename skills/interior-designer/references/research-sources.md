## Research hygiene
1. Prefer the applicable authority having jurisdiction and current official standards over blogs or social posts.
2. For product claims, prefer manufacturer datasheets and installation manuals for the exact model.
3. For prices and availability, record seller, city, date checked, delivery, installation, tax and warranty separately.
4. Distinguish legal requirements, voluntary standards, manufacturer recommendations and design preferences.
5. Never infer compliance from a product badge or generic checklist.
6. If authoritative material is unavailable, say so and seek qualified local advice; do not invent clause numbers or thresholds.
