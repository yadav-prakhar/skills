# Lighting audit workflow

1. Inventory every existing light visible or explicitly reported. Record location, type, control, direction and known power source.
2. Mark uncertain wiring, junctions, mounting surfaces and switch control as UNKNOWN / VERIFY.
3. Map activities and the required ambient, task, accent and decorative layers.
4. Identify dark zones, glare, shadows, colour mismatch and controls that are inconvenient.
5. Propose each new fixture with function, location, mount, power, switch/control, glare, maintenance and installer requirements.
6. Check interactions with wardrobes, wall panels, curtains, doors, fans and access panels. If joinery hides a current source, state the consequence and replacement plan.
7. Compare options by output, distribution, CCT, CRI, dimming, driver, dimensions, warranty and installed cost; verify manufacturer data.
8. Present what exists separately from what is proposed. Flag all site/electrical verification.
