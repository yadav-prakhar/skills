# Space-planning workflow

1. Establish room boundary, openings, fixed obstructions and measured dimensions.
2. List users, activities, furniture, storage and accessibility needs.
3. Place primary activities and large furniture first; keep fixed services visible.
4. Draw furniture footprints to scale only when dimensions are available; mark estimates.
5. Test door swings, drawers, wardrobe shutters, chair movement, window access, curtains, cleaning and service access.
6. Compare layouts on function, circulation, capacity, daylight, privacy, flexibility and cost.
7. Check delivery route and assembly access for large items.
8. Identify unknowns that could invalidate the preferred layout.
9. Recommend one layout with reasons and retain a fallback if a critical measurement is unknown.
