# Visual redesign workflow

1. Select source image(s) and camera viewpoint.
2. Write a geometry lock for walls, floor, ceiling, openings, fixed joinery, beams, columns and perspective.
3. Write an existing-condition lock: list each object and whether it must remain, may move, may be modified or may be removed.
4. List proposed additions with exact placement relative to visible anchors.
5. Specify material, colour, texture, lighting and scale.
6. List prohibited inventions: unconfirmed coves, outlets, windows, doors, fixtures, architectural details or hidden services.
7. Generate one controlled concept at a time.
8. QA against source: geometry, camera, retained objects, approved changes, scale, lighting, materials and missing constraints.
9. Disclose mismatches; never call a distorted concept a faithful visualization or measured drawing.
